# Summary

Aditya Bholla is an Analytics Engineer and Data Engineer focused on building enterprise data platforms, governed semantic models, AI-powered analytics products, and decision-support systems.

His work sits at the intersection of data engineering, analytics engineering, business intelligence, and applied AI. He has hands-on experience designing ETL/ELT pipelines, lakehouse and warehouse architectures, semantic models, analytics products, RAG systems, AI agents, and automation workflows.

# What Aditya Does

Aditya builds systems that transform fragmented operational and business data into reliable analytical products and AI-ready decision layers.

His work commonly includes:

- Designing end-to-end data pipelines and integration workflows
- Building governed semantic models and reusable business logic
- Creating enterprise analytics products and executive reporting
- Developing AI agents that reason over governed enterprise data
- Implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation, intent routing, prompt engineering, and evaluation controls
- Modeling entities and relationships for reusable analytics and application workflows
- Automating recurring operational intelligence and decision-support processes

# Current Focus

Aditya's current focus is analytics engineering and applied AI for enterprise operations. He is particularly interested in systems where data engineering creates reliable analytical foundations, semantic layers provide governed business context, AI agents interact with trusted enterprise data, and automation turns recurring analysis into repeatable operational workflows.

# Technical Profile

Aditya works across SQL, Python, TypeScript, Microsoft Fabric, Snowflake, Palantir Foundry, Power BI, dbt, Azure, AWS, PostgreSQL, Microsoft Copilot Studio, RAG, AI agents, vector databases, FastAPI, React, and Next.js.

# Working Style

Aditya approaches technical problems by connecting business context with system design. Rather than treating dashboards, data pipelines, semantic models, and AI agents as isolated deliverables, he prefers to design them as connected layers of the same decision system.

# Professional Identity

**Analytics Engineering + Data Systems + Applied AI**

He is most interested in roles and projects where he can build intelligent data products that combine reliable engineering foundations with analytics and AI.
