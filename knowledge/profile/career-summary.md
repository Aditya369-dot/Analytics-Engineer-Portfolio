# Summary

Aditya's career has progressed from business intelligence and analytics into analytics engineering, enterprise data platforms, and applied AI.

He began in sports analytics, moved into enterprise business intelligence and data engineering in public transit, and now works on large-scale operational analytics, semantic modeling, data platforms, and AI-enabled decision systems in the utilities industry.

# Career Trajectory

## 2020–2021 — Pro Football Focus

Aditya began his professional analytics career at Pro Football Focus as a Business Intelligence Analyst. This stage established his foundation in analytics, Python, data quality, and sports-focused data products.

## 2021–2024 — CapMetro / SOAL Tech

Aditya moved into enterprise business intelligence at CapMetro. This stage moved his career toward cloud data platforms, reusable data models, and analytics engineering.

## 2024–Present — PG&E

Aditya's career progressed into enterprise analytics engineering at PG&E, bringing together data engineering, analytics engineering, semantic modeling, business intelligence, and applied AI.
